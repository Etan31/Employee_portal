# Nexus Employee Portal

## Register
product

## Purpose and users
One internal portal for employees, managers, HR administrators, support agents and system administrators. The blueprint in `docs/Nexus_Employee_Portal_Blueprint.md` defines the product boundaries.

## Design principles
- Preserve the existing employee layout and shared navigation patterns.
- Show only authorized workspaces and actions; explain unavailable features honestly.
- Keep confidential records scoped to their business owners.
- Use the existing Nexus tokens, typography and accessible form controls.
- Favor concise task-focused copy over decoration or marketing language.
